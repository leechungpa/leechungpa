### Short Bio

Chungpa Lee is a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Statistics and Data Science at Yonsei University, where he is jointly advised by Professors [Joseph Kim](https://yonsei.elsevierpure.com/en/persons/joseph-ht-kim/) (Statistics) and [Jy-yong Sohn](https://itml.yonsei.ac.kr/professor/) (Machine Learning), and has worked closely with Professor [Kibok Lee](https://ml.yonsei.ac.kr/professor). Early in his Ph.D. program, he spent two years at an early-stage startup led by Professors [Jongho Im](https://www.yd3sl.com/#people) and Joseph Kim. He later spent a year as a visiting researcher at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, working with Professor [Kangwook Lee](https://kangwooklee.com/). He is currently a visiting student at the Allen School of Computer Science & Engineering at the University of Washington, working with Professor [Sewoong Oh](https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~sewoong/).
